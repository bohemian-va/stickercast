Why neurotoxin ads are harder than they look

Anti-wrinkle injections are one of the most searched aesthetic treatments, and one of the easiest to get wrong in Google Ads.

Prescription-drug rules

Neurotoxins are prescription drugs. Google only lets certified advertisers use prescription-drug terms in keywords, ads or ad landing pages, and med spas generally don't qualify for that certification.

Health Canada limits

In Canada, advertising a prescription drug to the public is limited to its name, price and quantity. Health Canada has flagged clinic before-and-after photos and benefit testimonials for these products.

Training and job traffic

“Injector course”, “certification” and “injector jobs” searches burn budget fast if they aren't blocked.

Lifetime value goes unmeasured

A client who rebooks several times a year is worth far more than the first visit. Most clinics judge ads on the first appointment alone.

Questions clinic owners ask

Can we bid on searches that include a drug brand name?

Not without Google's prescription-drug certification, which is aimed at pharmacies, telemedicine providers and manufacturers. In practice we target the concern and treatment searches around it, which still reach people looking for anti-wrinkle treatment.

How much should we spend on ads?

It depends on your city and treatments. Most single-location clinics start somewhere between $1,000 and $3,000 a month in ad spend. On the callback we'll look at search volume in your area and give you a number with reasoning behind it, not a guess.

Do we have to sign a contract?

No. Management is month to month, and the first month is free. Cancel any time and keep the account.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. It's created in your clinic's name and billed to your card. We're added as managers, and you can remove us whenever you like.

What do we need to provide?

Your treatment list, prices you're comfortable advertising, service area, and access to your website so we can add conversion tracking. We handle the rest.
