What goes wrong

Why neurotoxin ads are harder than they look

Anti-wrinkle injections are one of the most searched aesthetic treatments, and one of the easiest to get wrong in Google Ads.

⚠

Prescription-drug rules

Neurotoxins are prescription drugs. Google only lets certified advertisers use prescription-drug terms in keywords, ads or ad landing pages, and med spas generally don't qualify for that certification.

💰

Health Canada limits

In Canada, advertising a prescription drug to the public is limited to its name, price and quantity. Health Canada has flagged clinic before-and-after photos and benefit testimonials for these products.

🔍

Training and job traffic

“Injector course”, “certification” and “injector jobs” searches burn budget fast if they aren't blocked.

📊

Lifetime value goes unmeasured

A client who rebooks several times a year is worth far more than the first visit. Most clinics judge ads on the first appointment alone.
