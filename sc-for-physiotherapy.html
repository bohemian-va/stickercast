Where the budget leaks

Three things that quietly cost physiotherapy practices money

01

Your ads compete with clinic directories

Search "physiotherapy near me" in most Canadian cities and the first results are directory sites and aggregators bidding on your name's category. They capture the click, then sell that lead back to clinics. Structured properly, you outrank them on the searches that matter.

02

You're bidding on "physiotherapy" instead of conditions

Nobody wakes up wanting physiotherapy. They wake up with a sore back, a torn rotator cuff, or a knee that won't bend after surgery. Condition-level keywords convert far better and cost less, because fewer clinics think to bid on them.

03

Direct billing is your best ad copy and it's not in your ads

For a patient choosing between two clinics, "we direct bill your insurer" removes the single biggest friction point. It belongs in the headline, not buried on your fees page.

Compliance is built in, not bolted on

Physiotherapy advertising is governed by your provincial regulatory college — in Alberta that's the College of Physiotherapists of Alberta. Claims about outcomes, testimonials and comparative statements all carry rules. Every campaign is vetted against them before it runs.

FAQ

Physiotherapy advertising questions

What does Google Ads cost for a physiotherapy clinic?

+

Industry benchmark data puts physical therapy at $4.95 per click with a 15.35% conversion rate, giving an average cost per lead of $32.79 USD. That's roughly half the healthcare average of $66.02. For most single-location physio clinics, a starting ad budget of $800–$2,000 per month is realistic.

Is physiotherapy a good fit for Google Ads?

+

It's one of the best in healthcare. Across 16 specialties benchmarked, physical therapy has the third-lowest cost per lead and the third-highest conversion rate. The reason is intent: someone searching for help with a specific injury is ready to book, not researching.

Should I bid on condition keywords or 'physiotherapy near me'?

+

Both, in separate ad groups. "Physiotherapy near me" has volume but heavy competition from directories. Condition keywords — rotator cuff, sciatica, ACL rehab, vestibular — cost less, convert better, and let you write ad copy that speaks to the actual problem.

How do you handle College advertising rules?

+

Every campaign is built and monitored against your provincial college's advertising standards, plus Google's healthcare policies. That covers outcome claims, testimonials, comparative language and superlatives — the areas where physio advertising most often runs into trouble.
