The problem

Most practices aren't losing on Google. They're losing on the arrangement.

Your click costs are what the auction says they are. What quietly erodes the return is how the work gets priced, who owns the account, and whether anyone can tell you what happened last month.

%

You're charged more for spending more

The standard model is 15–20% of ad spend. Double your budget for a busy quarter and the fee doubles for identical work. You get penalised exactly when you are trying to grow.

$8,000/mo at 18% = $1,440 in fees
?

Nobody will quote you a price

Most healthcare marketing agencies will not publish a rate anywhere on their site. That is not custom pricing. It is sizing you up before quoting, and it means two similar practices pay very different amounts.

Typical band: $1,200–$5,000/mo, quote only
👁

You can't see what the ads did

Plenty of practices know patients arrive from Google because the front desk asks them. Almost none can say how many, which campaign, or what each one cost. Without that, every budget decision is a guess.

The most common finding in our audits

FAQ

Questions worth asking any agency

Is the first month really free?

+

Yes. We build the campaigns, set up conversion tracking and run the account for a full month before you pay us anything. Billing starts in month two. The one cost during the free month is Google's — you pay Google directly for clicks, and none of that comes to us. Month one is setup plus Google's learning period, so it is the month with the least to show; we would rather not charge for it.

How much does Stickercast cost?

+

Two plans. Practice is $497 per month for one campaign at a single location. Multi is $997 per month for unlimited campaigns across up to three locations. Both are flat fees in US dollars that do not change with your ad budget. Your Google ad spend is billed separately by Google and goes entirely to Google.

Why a flat fee instead of a percentage of ad spend?

+

Percentage-of-spend pricing gives an agency a reason to push your budget higher, and it charges you more for the same work as you grow. A flat fee means we get paid to make the budget convert, not to make it bigger.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

+

You do. Campaigns are built inside your own Google Ads account, so every keyword, conversion record and year of performance history belongs to you. If you leave, there is nothing to migrate and nothing held back.

What does Google Ads cost for a medical practice?

+

It varies sharply by specialty. Across 3,542 US healthcare campaigns, the average cost per click is $5.64 and the average cost per lead is $66.02. Dermatology is among the most efficient at $4.90 per click and $18.54 per lead, while general dentistry runs $7.03 per click and $84.77 per lead.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

+

No. Stickercast is month to month with 30 days notice and no setup fee.

Which practices do you work with?

+

Independent practices rather than hospital systems. We build around dermatology, physical therapy and dental, and we work with other outpatient specialties where the campaign mechanics are similar.
