Why we're different

Four things that actually separate us.

Not positioning. Each one is a specific, checkable difference in how the arrangement works.

01 — PRICING

A flat fee that never moves

$497 or $997 a month, whatever your ad budget is. Triple your spend for a busy season and the fee stays exactly the same. The percentage model charges you more for identical work every time you grow — and gives your agency a reason to push the budget up.

Industry norm: 15–20% of spend, quote only
02 — SPEED

Live in 3 business days

Audit and architecture on day one. Ad copy, conversion tracking and landing pages on day two. Live on day three. Most agencies take four to six weeks of discovery calls and onboarding decks before a single ad runs — weeks you are paying for.

Industry norm: 4–6 weeks to first impression
03 — OWNERSHIP

Your account. Your data. Permanently.

Campaigns are built inside your own Google Ads account. Every keyword, conversion record and year of performance history belongs to the practice. Leave whenever you like and nothing moves, nothing gets withheld, nothing has to be rebuilt from scratch somewhere else.

Common practice: agency-owned accounts you can't take
04 — ATTENTION

Reviewed every day, so nothing gets missed

Search terms, wasted spend, budget pacing, disapprovals and tracking health are checked daily. On the standard monthly cadence a single bad keyword can burn budget for four straight weeks before anyone opens the account. Most of what we fix is found between reviews that never would have happened.

Industry norm: one check-in per month

FAQ

Questions worth asking any agency

Is the first month really free?

+

Yes. We build the campaigns, install conversion tracking and run the account for a full month before you pay us anything. Billing starts in month two. You pay Google directly for ad clicks during the free month — that is the only cost, and none of it comes to us.

How much does Stickercast cost?

+

Practice is $497 per month for one campaign at a single location. Multi is $997 per month for unlimited campaigns across up to three locations. Flat fees in US dollars that never change with your ad budget. Your Google ad spend is billed separately by Google.

How fast can my campaign go live?

+

Three business days from the point we have account access and your service list. Day one is the audit and campaign architecture, day two is ad copy, tracking and landing pages, day three is launch.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

+

You do. Campaigns are built inside your own Google Ads account, so every keyword, conversion record and year of performance history belongs to the practice. If you leave, there is nothing to migrate and nothing held back.

How often is my account actually looked at?

+

Every day. Search terms, wasted spend, bids, budget pacing and conversion tracking get checked daily rather than at a monthly review. The standard agency cadence is a monthly check-in, which means a bad keyword can spend for four weeks before anyone notices.

Why a flat fee instead of a percentage of ad spend?

+

Percentage-of-spend pricing gives an agency a reason to push your budget higher, and charges you more for identical work as you grow. A flat fee means we get paid to make the budget convert, not to make it bigger.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

+

No. Month to month with 30 days notice, and no setup fee.

Which practices do you work with?

+

Independent practices rather than hospital systems. We build dedicated campaigns around dermatology, physical therapy, dental, family and primary care, and ophthalmology and optometry, and take on other outpatient specialties — chiropractic, med spas, urgent care and similar — where the campaign mechanics are similar.

Want us to take a look at your account?

Leave your name and number. We'll call you back, go through your Google Ads account with you, and tell you plainly what we'd change — what's wasting money, whether the tracking works, and what your cost per patient should be. No charge and no obligation, whatever you decide afterwards.
