Compliance-aware PPC

Healthcare ads need more than good copy. They need guardrails.

Medical and dental advertising can be governed by platform rules, provincial colleges, and health product advertising requirements. Stickercast builds compliance review into the campaign workflow.

Professional-body review

For Alberta medical clinics, we consider CPSA advertising expectations. For other provinces or professions, we map the relevant college guidance before campaigns go live.

Google policy review

We review health-related ad copy, landing page language, personalization limits, and restricted medical terms before launching.

Human approval for sensitive edits

The AI can flag and draft changes, but compliance-sensitive ad copy is routed for human review before publication.

This is not legal advice. Regulated professionals remain responsible for ensuring their advertising meets their own college, jurisdiction, and platform requirements.
