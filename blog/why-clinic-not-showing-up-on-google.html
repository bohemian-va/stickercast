STICKERCAST BLOG | SEO · Visibility | June 2026 Why Your Walk-In Clinic Isn't Showing Up on Google Six reasons patients can't find your clinic online — and exactly what to fix first. Most clinics have at least three of these problems without knowing it. A patient is sitting at home on a Sunday morning with a sore throat. They open Google, type "walk-in clinic open near me," and choose from the first three results they see. If your clinic isn't in those results, that patient goes somewhere else — and you never knew they were looking. Here are the six most common reasons — and what to do about each one. Reason 1: You're not running Google Ads When someone searches "walk-in clinic near me" on Google, the first 2–4 results are almost always paid ads — not the organic listings below them. If you're not advertising, you're invisible to that first screen entirely. Organic search results can take 6–12 months of consistent SEO work to rank well for competitive healthcare keywords. Google Ads puts you at the top immediately. The fix: Run a targeted Search campaign for your highest-value keywords — "walk-in clinic [your city]", "clinic open [day]", "family doctor accepting patients". Start with $300–600/month in ad spend. Reason 2: Your Google Business Profile is incomplete or wrong Google Maps results — the three listings with a map that appear for local searches — are driven almost entirely by your Google Business Profile. If your profile has wrong hours, no photos, or a sparse description, Google shows you less and lower. The most damaging issue is incorrect hours. If Google shows you as open when you're closed, patients arrive at the wrong time, leave frustrated, and often leave a 1-star review. The fix: Log into business.google.com and verify every field — especially hours for each day of the week. Add at least 10 photos. Write a 200–300 word description using the words patients actually search for. Reason 3: Your star rating is pushing patients away Even if you do appear in search results, a low star rating causes patients to scroll past you before they ever click. Below 4.0 stars, a significant portion of patients actively choose an alternative. Below 3.5, you're losing the majority. Research insight: A rating increase from 3.1★ to 4.0★ is estimated to improve ad-to-visit conversion by 25–40%. At $11 per click, that's the difference between $37 and $22 per patient. The fix: Train front desk staff to ask satisfied patients for a Google review at checkout. Set up a QR code at reception. Respond to every review within 48 hours. Reason 4: You're targeting the wrong keywords This is the most expensive mistake in Google Ads. It happens in two directions: too broad (bidding on "doctor" attracts people searching for medical schools) or too narrow (over-specifying to the point your ads never show). The sweet spot: intent-specific, geo-qualified keywords like "walk-in clinic open Sunday [city]" or "no appointment doctor [neighbourhood]". The fix: Review your Search Terms report in Google Ads monthly. Any search term with zero conversions after 20+ clicks is a candidate for a negative keyword. Reason 5: Your website is slow or broken on mobile Over 70% of healthcare searches happen on a mobile device. If your website takes more than 3 seconds to load on a phone, Google penalizes your organic ranking and patients bounce before they find your phone number. This affects Google Ads too. Google's Quality Score factors in your landing page experience — a slow mobile site raises your cost per click and lowers your ad position. The fix: Test your site at pagespeed.web.dev. Aim for a score above 80 on mobile. If you score below 50, it may be worth a full rebuild. Reason 6: Your ads run at the wrong times If your ads run 24/7 but your clinic is only open certain hours, you're paying for clicks from people who arrive at your website, see you're closed, and leave. Worse, your performance metrics look weak compared to competitors running ads only during their open hours. The fix: Set an ad schedule that matches your clinic hours. If you're open Sundays 9am–2pm, run ads from 8am–1:30pm. Pause ads outside those windows entirely.
