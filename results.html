Calgary Walk-In Clinic Sunday Campaign

A focused campaign for a Calgary primary care clinic launching Sunday walk-in hours. Built in 3 business days, monitored continuously, and reviewed against Alberta medical advertising expectations.

6.8%

campaign click-through rate

$11

average cost per walk-in

3 days

audit to live campaign

The challenge

The clinic had new Sunday availability but patients did not know to search for it. The goal was to capture high-intent local searches while keeping spend small and avoiding risky medical claims.

The Stickercast approach

  • Built specific ad groups for walk-in, Sunday, and family medicine intent.
  • Used radius targeting around Calgary instead of broad provincial targeting.
  • Paused weak terms and shifted budget toward queries with strong CTR.
  • Reviewed claims and wording against healthcare advertising expectations.

Your campaign does not need a huge budget to be measurable.

It needs tight targeting, clean tracking, and fast action when something starts wasting spend.
