FAQ

About specialties

Why does the specialty change how the campaign is built?

+

Because the economics are completely different. A dermatology lead costs a fifth of a dental lead. Some specialties book by phone, others by form. Some have an obvious high-value procedure to bid harder on, others don't. Applying the same keyword strategy across all of them wastes budget in one direction or the other.

What if my specialty isn't listed?

+

We take on other outpatient specialties where the campaign mechanics are similar — chiropractic, med spas, urgent care and more. Request a callback and we'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit before you commit to anything.

Do the dedicated pages cost anything to look at?

+

No. Each one lays out what real data we have for that specialty, the keywords worth bidding on, and how we structure the account — free to read whether or not you ever contact us.
