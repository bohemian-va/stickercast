What makes filler advertising different

Higher ticket, more research, and more comparison between clinics.

⚠

Long research phase

People read about filler for weeks before they book. Ads aimed at early-stage searches spend a lot for few consultations.

💰

Area-specific demand

Lip, cheek, jawline, chin and under-eye are different searches with different buyers. One generic ad fits none of them.

🔍

Dissolving and correction searches

“Filler gone wrong” and dissolving searches can be good business, or a liability. It needs a deliberate decision, not an accident.

📊

Expensive clicks in big cities

Toronto and Vancouver filler clicks are costly. Without tight targeting, budget runs out by mid-month.

FAQ

Questions clinic owners ask

Can we advertise brand names like Juvéderm or Restylane?

+

Dermal fillers are medical devices, not prescription drugs, so the rules are different from neurotoxin injections, but ads still go through Google's healthcare review. We'll check what your clinic can use before launch.

How much should we spend on ads?

+

It depends on your city and treatments. Most single-location clinics start somewhere between $1,000 and $3,000 a month in ad spend. On the callback we'll look at search volume in your area and give you a number with reasoning behind it, not a guess.

Do we have to sign a contract?

+

No. Management is month to month, and the first month is free. Cancel any time and keep the account.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

+

You do. It's created in your clinic's name and billed to your card. We're added as managers, and you can remove us whenever you like.

What do we need to provide?

+

Your treatment list, prices you're comfortable advertising, service area, and access to your website so we can add conversion tracking. We handle the rest.
