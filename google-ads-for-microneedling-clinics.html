Why microneedling ads underperform

The treatment name and the way people search for it don't match.

Searching the problem, not the treatment

Many potential clients search “acne scar treatment” or “large pores”, not “microneedling”. Treatment-only keywords miss them.

Home dermaroller traffic

Dermaroller and derma pen searches are mostly people buying devices, not booking clinics.

One-off sessions

Results need a series, and ads that sell one session leave revenue and results on the table.

Add-on confusion

RF microneedling, PRP add-ons and exosomes each need their own explanation. Mixed together, ads get vague.

Questions clinic owners ask

Does targeting concerns like acne scars cost more?

Concern searches are often cheaper than treatment-name searches and reach people earlier. We test both and move budget to whichever brings bookings at a lower cost.

How much should we spend on ads?

It depends on your city and treatments. Most single-location clinics start somewhere between $1,000 and $3,000 a month in ad spend. On the callback we'll look at search volume in your area and give you a number with reasoning behind it, not a guess.

Do we have to sign a contract?

No. Management is month to month, and the first month is free. Cancel any time and keep the account.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. It's created in your clinic's name and billed to your card. We're added as managers, and you can remove us whenever you like.

What do we need to provide?

Your treatment list, prices you're comfortable advertising, service area, and access to your website so we can add conversion tracking. We handle the rest.
