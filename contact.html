Free audit

Find out what your ad account is actually doing.

No pitch deck, no discovery call before anyone will tell you anything. We open the account, read the search terms, check whether the tracking works, and write you a plain-English report.

01

What we look at

Search terms you're paying for but shouldn't be. Whether conversion tracking fires on calls and forms, or fires on the wrong thing. Your cost per lead against the US median for your specialty. Bid strategy, match types, geo settings, and where the landing page loses people.

02

What you get back

A written document, in 2–3 business days, listing what's leaking money and the three changes we'd make first. It's yours regardless of what you decide about working with us.

03

What it costs

Nothing, and there's no obligation attached. If the answer is that your account is in decent shape and doesn't need an agency, we'll tell you that. And if you do go ahead, your first month of management is free on top — you pay Google for clicks, nothing to us until month two.

FAQ

About the audit

What do I get in the free audit?

+

A written review of your Google Ads account: which search terms are wasting money, whether conversion tracking is working, how your cost per lead compares to US benchmarks for your specialty, and the three changes we would make first. Yours to keep and act on whether you hire us or not.

What if I'm not running Google Ads yet?

+

Then we send you a build plan instead: the keywords worth bidding on in your area, realistic cost per click and cost per lead for your specialty, and what monthly budget would be sensible to start with.

How long does it take?

+

Two to three business days from when we get access.

Do I have to give you account access?

+

For an audit of an existing account, yes: read-only access to your Google Ads account is enough. We never need your password. If you'd rather not, send screenshots of your search terms report and we'll work from those.
