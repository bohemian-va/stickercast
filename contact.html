FAQ

About the callback

What happens on the callback?

+

We look at your Google Ads account with you on the phone and tell you what we would change: which search terms are wasting money, whether conversion tracking is actually working, and how your cost per lead compares to the US median for your specialty. Fifteen minutes is usually enough.

Is there really no cost?

+

None, and no obligation. If your account is in decent shape and doesn't need an agency, we will say so. If you do go ahead afterwards, the first month of management is free as well — billing starts in month two.

What if I'm not running Google Ads yet?

+

Then we talk through what it would cost: the keywords worth bidding on in your area, realistic cost per click and cost per lead for your specialty, and a sensible starting budget. No account needed.

Why do you ask for so little?

+

Because everything else is quicker to ask on the phone than to type into a form. We ask for your practice name so we can look you up and arrive at the call already knowing something about your market — everything else we can cover in two minutes on the phone.

Do I have to give you account access?

+

Not to get the callback. If you want us to look inside the account, read-only access is enough and you revoke it in two clicks whenever you like. We never need your password.

When will you call?

+

Within one business day, in the time window you pick. If we miss you we will email and try again rather than calling repeatedly.
