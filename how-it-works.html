Campaign Architecture & Ad Copy

Once you approve the plan, we build your full campaign structure. This is where practice-specific expertise matters — generic agency templates don't understand how patients search for walk-in care.

Ad group structure

Separate groups for: urgent walk-in, specific services (e.g. Sunday hours), family physician, and branded terms

Keyword strategy

Tightly matched keywords, negative keyword lists to block irrelevant traffic (e.g. veterinary, dental, pharmacy)

Ad copy

Responsive search ads with practice-specific headlines. You review and approve before anything goes live.

Extensions & targeting

Call extension, location extension, sitelinks. Radius targeting around your practice. Ad schedule matching your open hours.

Common Questions

Things clinics ask before signing up

Do I need an existing Google Ads account?

+

No. We create a new account under your Google login, or we work with your existing account if you have one. All campaigns remain in your name and ownership at all times.

What's the minimum ad budget?

+

We can work with as little as $5/day for single-campaign setups, though $10–15/day is where we consistently see meaningful results for health practices. The Stickercast management fee is separate from your ad spend.

Who owns the campaign and data?

+

You do. Your Google Ads account, your campaigns, your data. If you ever cancel, we remove our manager access and your full campaign history stays in your account.

How does the AI actually optimize?

+

Stickercast connects to your Google Ads account via the official API. Our AI agent reads performance data hourly and makes approved optimization actions — adjusting bids, pausing underperforming keywords, updating negative keyword lists — based on rules and thresholds we configure during onboarding. Any structural change (new campaigns, new ad copy) requires your approval.

What if my Google rating is low?

+

We address this directly. A low Google rating (below 4.0★) significantly reduces the conversion rate of ad clicks into walk-ins. Stickercast includes a ratings strategy alongside your ad campaign — review response templates, a QR code reception card, and a monitoring system to track rating improvement over time.

Do you work with non-clinic businesses?

+

We work with health practices and local service businesses across North America. Our campaign structures are built specifically for healthcare — health practices, family physicians, specialists, and allied health — and outperform generic agencies in every market we enter.
