How it works

AI speed, human judgement, healthcare-aware guardrails.

Stickercast is designed for regulated health businesses where bad ad copy can waste money and create professional risk. The AI monitors and analyzes; humans vet compliance-sensitive changes.

Day 1 — Audit

We map patient intent, competitor ad coverage, clinic hours, location radius, landing page quality, phone tracking, and policy risk.

Day 2 — Build

Campaigns are split by intent: walk-in care, dental emergency, new patients, specialty service, or high-value procedure.

Day 3 — Launch

We connect conversion tracking, set budgets, add negative keywords, and launch with strict review rules for health claims.

What the AI monitors

Instead of waiting for a monthly report, Stickercast checks the signals that decide whether your ad budget is helping or leaking.

  • Search terms: irrelevant clicks, competitor terms, low-intent searches.
  • Budget pacing: when spend is too fast, too slow, or going to the wrong ad group.
  • Conversion quality: calls, forms, map clicks, and likely patient intent.
  • Policy risk: medical claims, targeting restrictions, and health-body advertising rules.
  • Opportunity: new keywords, better time slots, and high-performing locations.
