Constant analysis

What gets checked every working day

This is the difference that costs agencies the most to offer and clients the most to go without. A monthly cadence means a mistake runs for four weeks. Here is the actual checklist.

New search terms

Every term that triggered your ad, read daily. Irrelevant ones become negative keywords the same day instead of spending for another month.

Wasted spend

Clicks that cost money and produce nothing. Job seekers, price shoppers, students, people looking for free care. Caught early, they stay small.

Budget pacing

A daily budget exhausted by 11am means your afternoon searches never see your ad. Pacing is checked so spend lands when patients actually search.

Ad disapprovals

Healthcare ads get flagged for wording nobody expected. A disapproved ad is a dead campaign until someone notices. Same-day catch, same-day fix.

Conversion tracking health

A website change can silently strip the tracking tag, and the account keeps bidding blind. We verify it still fires daily.

Bid and position drift

A competitor raising bids can quietly push you off the first page. Visible in a day, not at the end of the month.

Call quality

On Multi, calls are recorded and reviewed so you can hear which ads produce real patients rather than wrong numbers and sales calls.

Landing page conversion

Clicks arriving but nobody booking means the page is the problem, not the keyword. Tracked separately so we fix the right thing.

Why cadence beats cleverness

The cost of any mistake in a Google Ads account is time multiplied by budget. A wasteful keyword caught on day one costs you a day. The same keyword caught at a monthly review has spent for thirty. Most of what we fix isn't clever — it is ordinary, and it is only valuable because somebody looked.

FAQ

Before you start

How fast can my campaign really go live?

+

Three business days from the point we have Google Ads account access and your list of services. Day one is the account review and campaign architecture, day two is ad copy, conversion tracking and landing pages, day three is launch and monitoring. The main thing that delays it is waiting on account access.

What do you need from me to start?

+

Read access to your Google Ads account if you have one, a list of the services you want patients for, your service radius, and your phone number and booking process. That is it. No discovery workshop, no brand questionnaire.

What does 'reviewed every day' actually mean?

+

Someone opens the account every working day and checks the same list: new search terms, wasted spend, budget pacing, bid performance, ad disapprovals and whether conversion tracking is still firing. Anything wrong gets fixed that day rather than waiting for a monthly review.

Why does review cadence matter so much?

+

Because the cost of a mistake is time multiplied by budget. A bad keyword found on day one costs you a day of spend. The same keyword found at a monthly review has spent for four weeks. Most of what we fix would never have been found on a monthly cadence.

Do I have to give you my account password?

+

No, and you should not give that to anyone. Google Ads has proper access sharing — you grant our manager account access from your own account, and you can revoke it in two clicks at any time.

What happens if I want to leave?

+

Thirty days notice. You revoke our access and everything stays exactly where it is, because it was always in your account. No migration, no export, no handover fee.
