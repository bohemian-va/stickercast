What goes wrong

Three things that cost physical therapy clinics money

In PT the gap between a good keyword and a wasteful one is unusually wide, because so many searches are people trying to avoid a clinic rather than find one.

🎯

Bidding on 'physical therapy near me' and stopping there

It's the obvious keyword, which is why it's the expensive one. Meanwhile the person searching 'lower back pain won't go away' is further from a decision but far cheaper to reach, and converts once the page addresses their actual problem.

Condition terms often cost 30–50% less per click
📋

No answer to the referral question

Direct access rules vary by state, and the first thing a prospective patient wonders is whether they need a doctor's note. If the ad and landing page don't answer that in the first screen, a meaningful share of clicks leave to go find out.

Direct access is permitted in all 50 states, with limits
💰

Treating every enquiry as worth the same

A post-surgical rehab course is worth many times a single assessment, but most accounts count both as one conversion. The bidding then optimises toward whatever converts most often rather than whatever is worth most.

Course value ranges roughly $400–$4,000

How we build it

How a physical therapy account should be structured

Live in three business days, then reviewed every working day after that.

1

One campaign per body region, not one for the clinic

Back, knee, shoulder, post-surgical. Each with its own budget and landing page, so a person with shoulder pain reads about shoulders rather than about your facilities.

2

Answer the referral question in the ad itself

If your state allows direct access, say so in the headline. It removes the single biggest reason people click away before booking.

3

Weight conversions by what they're worth

Assessment bookings and post-surgical enquiries get different conversion values, so the bidding chases the higher-value patient instead of the more frequent one.

4

Track calls, because rehab books by phone

Most PT enquiries start with a call about availability and insurance. If those aren't tracked, the account optimises on a fraction of the real data.

5

Then reviewed every working day

Condition keywords attract a lot of self-treatment searches. Negatives get added daily so 'exercises for sciatica at home' stops costing you money in a day, not a month.

FAQ

Physical therapy advertising questions

What does a physical therapy lead cost on Google Ads?

+

The US median is $32.79 per lead at $4.95 per click and a 15.4% conversion rate, making physical therapy the third most efficient of sixteen healthcare specialties benchmarked.

Which keywords work best for a physical therapy clinic?

+

Condition-level terms consistently beat clinic-level terms. Searches like 'lower back pain treatment' or 'ACL rehab near me' cost less per click than 'physical therapy near me' and convert better, because the searcher has already identified their problem.

Do patients need a physician referral to book physical therapy?

+

Some form of direct access is permitted in all fifty states, though the limits differ by state and by insurer. Because many prospective patients assume they need a referral, saying otherwise in the ad copy measurably improves click-to-booking rates where it applies.

How much should a PT clinic budget for Google Ads?

+

At $32.79 per lead, $1,500 a month produces roughly 45 enquiries in an average market. Set the number against how many new evaluation slots you can realistically fill each week rather than a target spend.
