What goes wrong

Why med spa ads waste money

Most aesthetic clinics that try Google Ads on their own run into the same handful of problems.

⚠

Price shoppers and job seekers

Broad keywords pull in searches for training courses, jobs, DIY kits and the cheapest deal in town. Clicks you pay for that will never book.

💰

Ads disapproved without warning

Prescription-drug and healthcare policies catch med spa ads constantly. A disapproved ad quietly stops spending and nobody notices.

🔍

No idea what's working

Without conversion tracking on the form and phone, you can't tell which treatment or keyword produced a booking.

📊

One campaign for everything

Injectables, laser and fillers have different buyers, prices and seasons. Lumped together, the budget drifts to whatever's cheapest to click.

FAQ

Questions clinic owners ask

Which treatments should we advertise first?

+

Usually the ones with the best margin and the most repeat visits. Injectables and laser hair removal packages tend to be the strongest starting points. We'll recommend based on what you offer and your local competition.

How much should we spend on ads?

+

It depends on your city and treatments. Most single-location clinics start somewhere between $1,000 and $3,000 a month in ad spend. On the callback we'll look at search volume in your area and give you a number with reasoning behind it, not a guess.

Do we have to sign a contract?

+

No. Management is month to month, and the first month is free. Cancel any time and keep the account.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

+

You do. It's created in your clinic's name and billed to your card. We're added as managers, and you can remove us whenever you like.

Do we need to build a landing page ourselves?

+

No. We design and build a dedicated landing page for your campaign, written for the treatment you're advertising and set up with conversion tracking. We can host and maintain it for you, or add it to your existing website.

What do we need to provide?

+

Your treatment list, prices you're comfortable advertising, service area, and access to your website so we can add conversion tracking. We handle the rest.
