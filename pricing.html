FAQ

Pricing questions

Is the first month really free?

+

Yes, on either plan. We build the campaigns, install conversion tracking and run the account for a full month before charging anything. Billing starts in month two. You pay Google directly for ad clicks during that month — that is the only cost.

What exactly do I get for $497 a month?

+

Campaign build inside your own Google Ads account, conversion tracking for calls and forms, a dedicated landing page we host and maintain, daily search-term and negative keyword management, ad copy written and tested, and a monthly report showing what each enquiry cost. One campaign, one location.

When should I be on the $997 plan?

+

When you have more than one service line that needs its own campaign and budget, or up to three locations. A dermatology practice running medical and cosmetic separately, or a therapy group with three clinics, belongs on Multi.

Is my Google ad spend included?

+

No. Google bills you directly for clicks and that money goes entirely to Google. Our fee covers management only. Most single-location practices start between $1,000 and $2,500 a month in ad spend.

Is there a setup fee or minimum term?

+

Neither. No setup fee, no onboarding charge, month to month with 30 days notice.

How does this compare to a percentage-of-spend agency?

+

At a typical 15-20%, a practice spending $5,000 a month pays $750 to $1,000 in fees and one spending $10,000 pays $1,500 to $2,000. Our fee stays at $497 or $997 regardless of budget.
