What the fee does not cover — stated plainly

  • Your Google ad spend. Billed by Google, straight to your card. None of it comes to us — including during the free first month.
  • Website rebuilds. We build and host the landing pages the ads point at. We don't rebuild your main practice site.
  • SEO, social media or email marketing. We do one thing. Paid search.
  • A guaranteed number of patients. Anyone guaranteeing that in healthcare search is guessing, and charging you for the guess.

FAQ

Pricing questions

Is the first month really free?

+

Yes. We build the campaigns, set up conversion tracking and run the account for a full month before you pay us anything. Billing starts in month two. The one cost during the free month is Google's — you pay Google directly for clicks, and none of that comes to us. Month one is setup plus Google's learning period, so it is the month with the least to show; we would rather not charge for it.

What exactly is included for $497 a month?

+

Campaign build or rebuild inside your own Google Ads account, conversion tracking for calls and form submissions, a dedicated landing page we host and maintain, keyword and search-term management, negative keyword work, ad copy and testing, and a monthly report showing what each enquiry cost. One campaign, one location.

When should I be on the $997 plan instead?

+

When you have more than one service line that needs its own campaign and budget, or up to three locations. A dermatology practice running medical derm and cosmetic as separate campaigns, or a therapy group with three clinics, belongs on Multi.

Is my Google ad spend included in the fee?

+

No. Google bills you directly for clicks and that money goes entirely to Google, not to us. Our fee covers the management. Most single-location practices start somewhere between $1,000 and $2,500 a month in ad spend, but the right number depends on your specialty and how many new patients you can take.

Is there a setup fee or a minimum term?

+

Neither. No setup fee, no onboarding charge, month to month with 30 days notice.

How does this compare to a percentage-of-spend agency?

+

At a typical 15-20% of ad spend, a practice spending $5,000 a month pays $750 to $1,000 in fees, and a practice spending $10,000 pays $1,500 to $2,000. Our fee stays at $497 or $997 no matter what the budget is.

What if I already have a Google Ads account running?

+

Then we audit it first, free, before you commit to anything. Often the fix is structural rather than a rebuild, and you should know which one it is before you pay anyone.
